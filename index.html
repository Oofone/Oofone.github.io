About

I am a PhD student in Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, working in the Speech and Language Lab under Prof. Chng Eng Siong. My research direction is noise-robust adaptation of joint speech–large language models for under-resourced languages.

Most of what I work on sits at the join between a speech encoder and a text LLM: how to align the two cheaply, how to keep that alignment from falling apart under noise, and how to make it hold for languages with very little annotated data. Recent work has been on multilingual instruction-following speech LLMs trained from ASR data alone, on disentangling discrete speech representations for robustness, and on code-switching ASR.

Before the PhD I spent four and a half years at Amazon as a software engineer, mostly on distributed back-end services. I moved back into research in 2024.

Research interests

  • Speech large language models — speech–text alignment, distillation, instruction following
  • Multilingual and low-resource speech understanding
  • Noise-robust ASR and discrete speech representations
  • Code-switching speech recognition
